Join NeuraSec

An open, collaborative, international research community. Whether you’re a student, researcher or faculty member, there’s a place for you here.

Open to collaboration
We welcome research collaborations, joint publications and scholarly discussion from around the world.

If you share our interest in AI security, deep learning, NLP or XAI, get in touch.

Ways to join

Research mentorship

Guidance on your first research paper, from topic selection to submission, under direct supervision of experienced members.

Who: BS/MS/PhD students with a strong ML or security background

Co-authorship

Contribute to active projects and earn co-authorship on peer-reviewed journal or conference papers.

Who: Students who can commit 3–6 months to a research task

Open-source tools

Help build NeuraSec's open research tooling — datasets, pipelines and benchmarks used by the wider community.

Who: Students strong in Python, ML engineering or full-stack development

Joint publications

Partner with us on papers for leading journals (Springer, Elsevier, IEEE) and venues such as NeurIPS, ICLR, CVPR and their workshops.

Who: Independent researchers and postdocs with active research tracks

Research discussions

Exchange ideas, review manuscripts or brainstorm new directions. No formal commitment needed.

Who: Anyone working where AI and security meet

Dataset & benchmark sharing

Share or co-develop datasets, benchmarks and evaluation frameworks for the AI security community.

Who: Researchers with specialised domain data or evaluation expertise

International advisor

Join our advisory board, help steer research direction and mentor early-career researchers. Light commitment, high impact.

Who: Faculty or senior researchers outside Pakistan with a proven publication record

Institutional partnership

A formal link between your institution and NeuraSec for student exchange, seminars and co-supervised research.

Who: Universities and labs interested in Pakistan–international collaboration

Guest seminars & talks

Give a virtual seminar on your research to NeuraSec members and our wider student community.

Who: Any researcher with an interesting topic to share

Applied research projects

Bring real-world problems in cybersecurity, AI or data science and we'll design and run rigorous research to address them.

Who: Companies and startups in security, fintech, health-tech or AI

Sponsored research

Fund a research direction aligned with your organisation's mission. Publications remain open-access and academically independent.

Who: Organisations seeking research impact with academic credibility

Consulting & advisory

NeuraSec expertise for AI security audits, model evaluation, threat analysis or technology assessment.

Who: Teams needing specialised AI/cybersecurity knowledge

Writing to us

The easiest way to start is an email to neurasec1@gmail.com. A few lines on each of these helps us reply usefully:

  1. Who you are. Your name, current position and institution (or the organisation you represent).
  2. What interests you. The area you would like to work on, such as AI security, NLP, explainable AI or AI in healthcare, and why.
  3. Where we can learn more. A CV, or links to your Google Scholar, ORCID, GitHub or LinkedIn profile.
  4. What you can offer. Your availability (roughly how many hours a week, and for how long) and any deadlines you are working to.
