Privacy

What this website stores, which other services it contacts, and how to ask for a change.

In short

This is a static website. It has no accounts, no forms, no advertising and no analytics, and it does not set cookies.

What stays in your browser

Three preferences are saved in your browser’s local storage so the site remembers them on your next visit. They never leave your device.

  • neurasec-theme: light or dark colour theme
  • neurasec-team-view: cards or list layout on the People page
  • neurasec-motion: whether animations are paused

Other services this site contacts

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. As host, GitHub receives the usual technical information about each request (for example your IP address); see GitHub’s privacy statement.
  • OpenStreetMap: the map on the Global Reach page loads map tiles from OpenStreetMap’s servers, which therefore see your IP address when you open that page. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.
  • Fonts, flag images and the map library are served from this site, not from third parties.
  • Links to LinkedIn, Google Scholar, ORCID, publishers and other sites take you away from this site; their own privacy policies apply there.

Information about people

The People pages show members’ names, roles, institutions, photographs and public profile links (and, for some people, a professional email address). Publication pages list the authors of papers. This information is published with the knowledge of the people concerned.

If you are listed and would like something corrected or removed — a photo, an email address, your whole entry — write to neurasec1@gmail.com and we will change it promptly.
