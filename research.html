What we work on

Research areas

Six connected domains addressing critical challenges in modern computing and intelligent systems, and the open-source tools we build along the way.

Cybersecurity

Intrusion detection for IoMT, IoT edge devices, CAN bus and UAV networks; adversarially robust IDS, IoT malware and phishing detection, vehicle authentication and LLM jailbreak defence.

AI & Deep Learning

Transformers, stacking ensembles, federated knowledge distillation, multi-agent LLM systems, edge AI and TinyML, and nature-inspired optimisation for detection, forecasting and decision support.

Explainable AI

Interpretable machine learning with SHAP-based diagnostics, attention visualisation and per-class analysis for transparent, trustworthy systems.

Natural Language Processing

Urdu–Roman Urdu transliteration, Urdu toxicity detection, social-media bot detection, multilingual infoveillance, chatbot intent recognition and LLM safety.

AI in Healthcare

Skin-lesion and lung-disease classification, wearable sleep staging, falls detection, hydration monitoring and smart-home monitoring for ageing in place.

Interdisciplinary Research

Generative AI in higher education, flood and wildfire monitoring, graph theory and network reliability, sports analytics, agricultural price forecasting and urban mobility.
